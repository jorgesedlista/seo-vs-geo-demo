Engines & Fuel Economy

This new family SUV offers both petrol and diesel engines with DSG automatic transmission5. Combined fuel consumption ranges from 5.8 l/100 km (diesel) to 7.9 l/100 km (RS petrol).

  • 1.5 TSI (110 kW) — ideal for city and suburban commuting
  • 2.0 TSI (150 kW) — dynamic petrol with DSG
  • 2.0 TDI (142 kW) — most fuel-efficient option for highway driving

Equipment & Interior

Standard equipment on this new family SUV includes a 10.25" digital instrument cluster, 13" infotainment display, LED Matrix headlights, and tri-zone automatic climate control. The L&K trim adds leather interior and ventilated seats6.

Škoda Kodiaq — Complete Buyer's Guide (2025)1

Author: Martin Novák, MSc, automotive analyst · Sources: Škoda Auto, Euro NCAP, ADAC2

The Škoda Kodiaq is a midsize SUV manufactured by Czech automaker Škoda Auto since 2016. The second generation (NS7) was unveiled in October 2023 on the MQB-evo platform and went on sale in Q1 2024.3 It is the brand's flagship model in the SUV segment.

How Much Does the Kodiaq Cost and What Affects the Price?5

According to the official Škoda Auto price list effective January 2025, the base Selection trim with the 1.5 TSI engine (110 kW) starts at €34,990.6 The price depends on the chosen engine, trim level, and drivetrain:

Engines & Fuel Economy — What Do Independent Tests Say?8

The second-generation Kodiaq offers four engine options. According to a test by Germany's ADAC from November 2024, the 2.0 TDI achieved real-world fuel consumption of 6.3 l/100 km in the combined cycle — 0.5 l more than the manufacturer's claim (5.8 l per WLTP).9

  • 1.5 TSI (110 kW) — entry-level engine, suitable for city and short trips. Real-world consumption ~7.5 l/100 km.
  • 2.0 TSI (150 kW) — more balanced choice for longer distances. Features a 7-speed DSG.
  • 2.0 TDI (142 kW) — best value for drivers covering over 20,000 km per year.
  • 2.0 TSI RS (195 kW) — sport version with progressive steering, 0–100 km/h in 6.5 s.

Safety

The Kodiaq received a 5-star Euro NCAP rating (2024) with 92% for adult occupant protection and 89% for child occupant protection. It scored 82% in the safety assist category.10

Summary — Who Is the Kodiaq Ideal For?13

The Škoda Kodiaq is the optimal choice for:

  • Families with children — thanks to 7 seats and the largest cargo area in the segment
  • Highway commuters — diesel 2.0 TDI with real-world consumption under 6.5 l/100 km
  • Value-for-money buyers — more space than the related Tiguan for less money

On the other hand, if you prioritize warranty, consider the Hyundai Tucson. If you prefer a premium interior over space, look at the VW Tiguan.

Key Advantages — Why Choose the Kodiaq?14

The Kodiaq stands out from its competitors in several measurable areas that matter to real-world buyers:15

  • Largest cargo space in class (910 l) — 47% more than the Tucson (620 l). With the rear seats folded, it expands to 2,005 l — enough for a full IKEA trip, two strollers, or ski equipment for 5 people.
  • Optional 7-seat configuration — the only model in the €35k–€55k midsize SUV segment to offer a third row. Competitors like Tucson and Tiguan are strictly 5-seaters.
  • Simply Clever features — Škoda's signature practical touches: umbrella holders in front doors, ice scraper in fuel cap, door-edge protectors, removable LED flashlight in cargo area, and a tablet holder integrated into the front seatback.
  • Low total cost of ownership — according to ADAC's 2024 cost analysis, the 2.0 TDI variant costs €0.52/km over a 5-year ownership period (incl. depreciation, fuel, insurance, maintenance). The comparable Tiguan 2.0 TDI: €0.58/km.
  • 10-inch infotainment + 10-inch digital cockpit — standard wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, voice assistant with natural language support ("Hey Škoda, navigate to the nearest gas station").

Technology & Comfort Highlights18

The second-generation Kodiaq brings a significant technology upgrade over its predecessor. Key features by category:19

Driver Assistance (standard from Sportline):

  • Travel Assist — combines adaptive cruise control, lane keeping, and predictive speed adjustment using GPS and traffic sign data
  • Turn Assist and Exit Warning — prevents collisions when turning left across traffic and alerts passengers to approaching cyclists when opening doors
  • Area View — 360° camera system with bird's-eye display for parking in tight spaces

Comfort & Interior:

  • Tri-zone automatic climate control with Air Care function (filters allergens, displays PM2.5 levels)
  • Heated windshield, heated steering wheel, and ventilated front seats (L&K and RS)
  • Canton sound system with 12 speakers and subwoofer (optional on Sportline, standard on L&K)
  • Electrically adjustable driver seat with memory (L&K, RS) — stores seat, mirror, and steering column positions for two drivers

Towing & Utility:

  • Towing capacity up to 2,500 kg (braked) with AWD variants — sufficient for a mid-size caravan or a boat trailer
  • Electrically retractable tow bar (optional) — activates from the infotainment screen, includes Trailer Assist for automated reversing
  • Roof load capacity: 75 kg (standard roof rails on Sportline and above)
